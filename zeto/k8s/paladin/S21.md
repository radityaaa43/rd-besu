## Rollback rule (c)
Until the new node of that party **submits its first tx** (including the S11 republish tx counts as its first tx): `scale sts paladin-node<N> -n rd-zeto-<p> --replicas=0`, re-create `rehearsal-no-9000-egress` (render from the S1 sha), `scale sts paladin-node<N> -n paladin --replicas=1`, republish nothing (old endpoint untouched). After the first tx: roll forward only (the signer nonce lives in the new DB: transaction_orchestrator.go:259-279), fix on the new node. Because the S11 republish is itself a tx, the safe rollback point is steps 3-7; after step 8 starts, roll forward.

## Outage per party (estimate, measured parts)
dump+restore 65-100 s same cluster (2.4 GB logical, 165 MB dump), bank-c cross-cluster measured 47 s; start 2-5 min (unmeasured, first start of a ~12-day-old DB); proofs + republish 3-5 min; ~10-12 min per party, ~45-50 min for four, plus freeze/unfreeze and VERIFY: about 90 minutes window, Lensa down about 45 minutes.

## Risks
Paladin pods have no resource limits (BestEffort): a node can be OOM-killed on a tight node (cluster 2). The first start may re-run migrations (autoMigrate true) on the copied DB. During the window the four nodes are cut one by one: new-to-old messaging does not work (old nodes cannot reach the new LB path and the new nodes' registry entries point to LB IPs until step 8), which is why Lensa stays frozen for the whole window. S5b (old validators) and S22 (delete old ns) are NOT part of this window.
